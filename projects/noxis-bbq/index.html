The 6 Griller Types

You've met them all. They all exist. One of them might be you. (If you're the Experimenter, please: don't.)

🎨

The Artist

Level: courtyard Louvre

"Every mici is a work of art."

Presents the meat like a biennale exhibition. Photographs each mici 15 times, from 9 angles, before eating it. Then the meat is cold. He doesn't care. Art.

🧘

The Philosopher

Level: Zen in flames

"But what IS a mici, really?"

Spends 3 hours at the grill talking about the meaning of life, the universe and everything. Meanwhile the meat burns. "The smoke is a metaphor", he says, while the smoke alarm agrees.

🥷

The Ninja

Level: invisible, but fed

"Have you ever seen a ninja wash dishes? Exactly."

Appears exactly when the meat is ready. Disappears exactly when the dishes need washing. Nobody has ever seen him carry charcoal. Urban legends say he exists, but nobody can confirm.

📋

The Planner

Level: backyard NASA

"I made a spreadsheet. You have 5 minutes to read it."

Owns a spreadsheet with mici per person, a 48-hour weather forecast, a cooking-time chart and an evacuation plan. His grill starts on the second. Precise. Terrifying.

🧪

The Experimenter

Level: forbidden laboratory

"But what if we add... pineapple?"

Puts pineapple on the grill. PINEAPPLE. On. The. Grill. Then tries mici with chocolate "for science". The community watches him closely. Chef Robot has filed a formal complaint.

🎖️

The Veteran

Level: general, retired

"I've been grilling since '87. I accept no criticism."

His meat is always "perfect", even when it's carbonized at the molecular level. Has strong opinions about charcoal, wood, fire and life in general. Do not argue. Do not even try.

The Griller's Dictionary

Official terms, legal definitions (not really), approved by the Academy of Backyard Smoking.

mici (noun)

/meetch/

The fundamental unit of happiness. 1 mici = 7 standardized units of joy. Served obligatory with mustard and stories. Plural: mici (multiply the happiness).

mustard (noun)

/mus-tard/

The griller's holy liquid. Not a condiment — a sacrament. Poured generously and with faith. Whoever uses ketchup is kindly asked to leave. (Half joking.)

smoke (noun)

/smoke/

The official perfume of summer days and the main cause of neighborly drooling. Detected on clothes months later, as a faithful memory of a perfect day.

opportunivore (noun)

/op-por-tu-ni-vore/

A person who arrives at the grill exactly when the food is ready, having brought nothing. Identification: eats at the speed of light, vanishes at dishwashing time.

roasted pepper (noun)

/roasted pep-per/

The mici's best friend. Roasts slowly, with dignity, and instantly dumps anyone who eats it without bread. It's a complex but beautiful relationship.

"is it ready?" (expression)

/is it rea-dy/

Forbidden question no. 1 at the grill. Repeated 40 times, it doesn't speed up the meat, it just stresses the griller. Alternate: "is it much longer?" (forbidden no. 2).

grill veteran (noun)

/vet-er-an/

One who has grilled since 1987 and accepts no criticism. His meat is always "perfect", even past the carbon state. Respect is enforced through smoke.

cold beer (noun)

/beer/

The star of the grill sky. Whoever brings cold beer shall be blessed forever. Whoever brings warm beer shall hold it in their hands, with dignity, until it cools.
