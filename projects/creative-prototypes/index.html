CHAPTER III — THE GALACTIC DANUBE CATFISH
SPECIMEN 003 — SILURUS GALACTICUS ALATUS Habitat: The Danube, between Mile 23 and the Andromeda Galaxy. Nyxer: „We'll sell it as a dragon!” Mondy: „THAT'S NOT A DRAGON! THAT'S A CATFISH! CHAPTER V — Z AND THE COSMIC WORM
SPECIMEN 005 — LUMBRICUS COSMICUS STELLARIS The laboratory attempts a dragon with a comet body, flame crest, horns, claws and whiskers. The horns and whiskers are using local coordinates!” Nyxer: „AND WHO'S PAYING TO SHIP THE HORNS?!” Z230: „SO THE DRAGON WASN'T EVEN A MUTANT?! IT WAS JUST ASSEMBLED WRONG?!” AREN: „Catalog update: anatomical realignment procedure in progress.” Z230: „IF IT GROWS A SECOND HEAD, I'M RETIRING!” Nyxar: „Confirmed! This is not a dragon. It's a cosmically modified earthworm!” Nyxer: „AN EARTHWORM?! AND WE INVESTED IN GENETIC RESEARCH FOR THIS?!” Mondy: „HEY! THAT'S BAIT FOR AREN'S CATFISH!” AREN: „Specimen 003 can eat Specimen 005.
